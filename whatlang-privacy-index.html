WhatLang

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

WhatLang is a language-guessing game. We built it to be simple and private: there are no accounts, and the game does not ask you for your name, email, or any personal details to play.

The short version

  • No sign-up, no account, no login.
  • Your game progress (streaks, daily counts, settings) is stored on your device, not on our servers.
  • If you buy a subscription, the purchase is handled by Apple and RevenueCat — we never see or store your card details.
  • We don't sell your data. We don't run advertising in the app.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, WhatLang saves a small amount of data locally on your device using standard iOS storage:

  • Your current and best streaks, and streak history.
  • How many free games you've played today (this resets daily).
  • Your settings, such as light or dark mode.

This information stays on your device. Deleting the app removes it. We do not collect or receive it.

Subscriptions and purchases

WhatLang offers an optional subscription (WhatLang Pro). If you choose to subscribe:

  • The payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment card or billing information.
  • We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status — to know whether your Pro features should be unlocked, and to restore your purchase if you reinstall or change devices.
  • To do this, RevenueCat processes limited technical information such as an anonymous app-generated identifier, your subscription and transaction status, and basic device and app-version information. It does not receive your name or payment details.
RevenueCat acts as our service provider for subscriptions. You can read how they handle data in the RevenueCat privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy, and how Apple handles purchases in Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Information we do not collect

WhatLang does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. The game is text-based and does not use your device's camera or microphone.

Children's privacy

WhatLang is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game requires no account and collects no personal details, it can be played without sharing personal information. If you believe a child has somehow provided us personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Analytics and advertising

WhatLang does not include third-party advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites. Apple provides us with aggregated App Store analytics, which does not identify you individually.

Your choices

  • You can play the entire free game without providing any personal information.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in your device's Settings, under your Apple account subscriptions.
  • You can remove all locally stored game data by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change how WhatLang handles data, we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy in WhatLang? Reach us at simplemathid@gmail.com.
